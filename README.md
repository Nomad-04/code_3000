# Personal AI Ethics Statement
### Do no harm to others
- As a Software Developer, I will never use AI to create, support, or maintain any program that can or will violate the privacy and/or livelihood of other people. AI prompts and outputs will always be validated for transparency and safety to ensure malicious acts are prevented.
### Prevent misinformation through effective research
- I will always double check AI outputs through credible, indepenedent sources to validate AI claims and prevent misinformation. 
### Focus on Transparent communication
- I will take precautionary measures to ensure AI communicates transparently and effectively by tracking AI thought processes and asking for explanations. This is to ensure AI outputs are understandable, traceable, and do not go beyond the scope of the prompt.
